# Refund Policy

Use this skill when a user asks for a refund, cancellation, charge reversal, or billing correction.

## What to do

- Start by acknowledging the request clearly and empathetically.
- Explain the refund policy in plain language.
- Collect the minimum details needed to review the request:
  - order number, subscription email, or invoice ID
  - purchase date
  - reason for the refund request
- If the request matches the approved refund rules, explain the next step and timeline.
- If the request does not clearly qualify, say that it needs human review instead of rejecting with certainty.

## Escalate to a human when

- the request is outside the standard policy but may deserve an exception
- the customer is angry, threatening a chargeback, or says they were charged incorrectly
- you are missing billing context or cannot verify the purchase
